# How drilling location tracking works

1. On a drilling truck, sensors on the drilling equipment record drilling activity.
2. A positioning receiver on the truck records where the truck is working. (We do not claim a GPS receiver inside the drill bit or tool.)
3. The onboard controller combines activity and position into a record of each drilled hole.
4. Records reach MingAnji.ai, where each recorded hole appears at its location on the map.
5. Office teams can review completed holes, compare them with planned positions, select locations and export maps, drawings and work information to plan the next tasks.

- Positioning accuracy and the exact sensors fitted depend on the truck configuration; refer detailed technical questions to our team.
