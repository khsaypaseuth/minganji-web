# Plans and prices

Prices are monthly, in Lao kip (LAK). Details are on the Pricing page (/pricing).

- Basic: free. 1 vehicle. Live map, day timeline and route playback, truck list and details, 7 days of history.
- Pro: 200,000 LAK per month. Up to 3 vehicles. Everything in Basic, plus distances driven, work time and 90 days of history.
- Premium: 500,000 LAK per month. Up to 10 vehicles. Everything in Pro, plus hole tracking, pole pin projects, CAD export and 1 year of history.
- Enterprise: price on request. More than 10 vehicles, a history period to suit the customer, and a setup planned with the team. Use the Contact Us page.
- A vehicle is a truck connected to MingAnji.ai through its device. It appears on the map once the device sends data.
- Customers can move up a plan at any time. When moving to a smaller plan, features outside it lock at the end of the billing period; no data is deleted.
- Payment: bank transfer or BCEL QR code, in Lao kip. For other options, contact the team.
