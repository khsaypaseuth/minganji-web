# Plans and prices

Prices are monthly. Each website language shows its own currency, and plans are billed in the currency shown. Details are on the Pricing page (/pricing).

| Plan | LAK (Lao) | USD (English) | THB (Thai) | VND (Vietnamese) | CNY (Chinese) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | 200,000 | 9 | 299 | 229,000 | 59 |
| Premium | 500,000 | 22 | 749 | 575,000 | 148 |

When answering, quote the price in the currency of the visitor's language.

- Basic: free. 1 vehicle. Live map, day timeline and route playback, truck list and details, 7 days of history.
- Pro: up to 3 vehicles. Everything in Basic, plus distances driven, work time and 90 days of history.
- Premium: up to 10 vehicles. Everything in Pro, plus hole tracking, pole pin projects, CAD export and 1 year of history.
- Enterprise: price on request. More than 10 vehicles, a history period to suit the customer, and a setup planned with the team. Use the Contact Us page.
- A vehicle is a truck connected to MingAnji.ai through its device. It appears on the map once the device sends data.
- Customers can move up a plan at any time. When moving to a smaller plan, features outside it lock at the end of the billing period; no data is deleted.
- Payment: in Laos, bank transfer or BCEL QR code. In other countries, the team confirms the payment method with the customer.
