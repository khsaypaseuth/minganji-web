# System features (five areas)

## 1. Truck and equipment monitoring
- Sensors and an onboard controller collect operating information.
- GPS location and tracking of connected trucks.
- Equipment operating hours and activity (for example engine and working-equipment activity).
- Truck status at a glance (for example online, idle or offline).

## 2. Mapping and field operations
- Drilling activity with recorded hole locations.
- Mapping of drilling locations.
- Truck tracking and daily movement on a map.
- Map layers / overlays, for example recorded holes, routes and planned positions.
- Monitoring work progress across sites.

## 3. Planning and exports
- Select and review mapped drilling locations.
- Summaries of completed and remaining work.
- Export maps, drawings and work information. (Specific file formats are not confirmed publicly — say that our team can confirm formats.)
- Prepare upcoming tasks based on completed work.

## 4. Staff management
- Staff records, work assignments and timesheets.
- Work time can be reviewed alongside field activity.
- Exact scope depends on configuration — suggest asking our team.

## 5. Business management
- Operational summaries, reports and management dashboards.
- One view across trucks, work and people to support management decisions.

## What managers can see (summary)
Truck locations and activity, recorded drilling locations, equipment operating hours, work progress, staff and timesheet summaries, and operational dashboards. Exact screens depend on the configuration.
