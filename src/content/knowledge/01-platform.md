# What MingAnji.ai is

- MingAnji.ai is an intelligent truck monitoring and business management platform, powered by Tian'An (the truck brand).
- Tian'An trucks can be equipped with sensors and an onboard controller that collect operating information.
- MingAnji.ai turns this information into maps, dashboards, reports and tools for planning and managing work.
- The flow: truck → sensors → onboard controller → MingAnji.ai → map → planning and management.
- It is designed so that field activity becomes visible to office teams and managers.
- Product capabilities and availability may vary by truck model, configuration and region.
