# Trucks we sell and equip (Tian'An)

Five categories:
1. Non-insulated bucket trucks — aerial access for maintenance, installation and inspection work.
2. Insulated bucket trucks — insulated aerial platforms for work near energised lines.
3. Mobile generator trucks — mobile power for sites, outages and temporary supply.
4. Drilling trucks — truck-mounted drilling, e.g. for pole holes and foundations.
5. Fire trucks — firefighting vehicles for emergency response.

- Sensor and controller options depend on the truck and its configuration. Not every truck model has identical equipment.
- No model names, specifications, capacities or certifications are published here. For specifications, refer visitors to the Contact Us page.
